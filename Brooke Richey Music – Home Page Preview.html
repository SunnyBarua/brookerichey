The Instrument Within You

It is serious. It is personal. It is your voice.

I uncovered a 15-year suppressed traumatic memory that kept me thinking small and in an addictive relationship with music. I did this with one thing: my voice.

Before recovering it, the only place I felt that I was really heard was through singing on stage. Anywhere else, I would avoid eye contact, mumble, speak in a gravelly low register, turn away while making a statement. I would even cover my mouth with my own hand.

Your voice is the tool and instrument that can heal your mind and body, transform your life and take your career where you want it to go.

What started as a mission to end generational trauma in my family turned into a fierce passion to help others find and develop their authentic voice, or like me, take it back and go beyond what I was conditioned to believe was possible.

Just three words hold the power to change your life: I am and no. How often do you use them, and are you aware of how you use them now?
